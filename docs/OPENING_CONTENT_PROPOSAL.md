# Opening Content — APPROVED BASELINE

The three caged opening animals and ten surviving SpliceMaster source packages are now approved as the initial game-content baseline. Exact numeric stats, expression probabilities and tutorial tuning remain implementation details to be proven later.

## Design purpose

The opening choice should immediately teach three things:

1. The base animal matters.
2. The same source material can express differently.
3. A starter can plausibly remain one of the player's treasured main creatures rather than being discarded for a strictly higher-tier replacement.

The lab animals also fit SplicePit's pastoral-biotech identity and plausibly remain in cages after the disaster.

---

# Opening base animals — LOCKED

## 1. Rabbit — speed / fragility / easy future testing

**Why it works**

- Visually readable and inherently slightly comic when turned into something monstrous.
- Fast/light body gives very different consequences from heavier starters.
- Rabbits remain extremely easy to source later, making the player's original rabbit unusually well-understood experimentally.
- Supports the important lesson that “common” does not mean “bad”.

**Natural tendencies**

- high mobility
- low mass
- strong hind limbs
- alert senses
- relatively fragile

## 2. Goat — balance / toughness / aggression

**Why it works**

- Pastoral identity fits the world perfectly.
- Naturally capable of ramming/climbing and already possesses horns, creating interesting structural interactions with additional anatomical material.
- Mid-sized and robust without being a generic all-round fantasy creature.

**Natural tendencies**

- solid structure
- good balance/footing
- head/ram capability
- moderate mass
- stubborn/aggressive behavioural potential

## 3. Pig — mass / durability / power

**Why it works**

- Strong contrast to Rabbit.
- Intelligent-for-an-animal without approaching human intelligence.
- Body mass, jaw strength and durability provide a natural heavy build.
- Visually excellent substrate for increasingly absurd modifications.

**Natural tendencies**

- high mass
- good vitality
- strong neck/jaw/body
- lower agility than Rabbit
- high food/metabolic burden

## Why not start with rare animals?

The starter is chosen from what the dead SpliceMaster happened to have in ordinary cages. Rare bases such as lions become desirable partly because obtaining equivalent animals for safe testing is difficult.

The player can turn one of these mundane starters into a late-game main creature if they choose.

# Initial source records/material — LOCKED

These are **source packages**, not literal one-gene-one-power upgrades. Each source has tendencies across the agreed biological classes and can express differently by attempt/base animal.

## 1. Lion — Predatory Suite

**Likely classes:** behavioural/neurological + anatomical + physiological

Possible expressions:
- stronger predatory drive
- increased aggression/commitment
- jaw/tooth development
- claw development where anatomy permits
- burst-power improvements

Risks:
- poor behavioural control
- metabolic cost
- unpredictable anatomical overexpression

## 2. Rhinoceros — Cranial Keratin / Impact Suite

**Likely classes:** anatomical + regulatory

Possible expressions:
- horn growth
- thickened skull/neck structures
- charging/impact capability

Variation can change horn number, size, placement and structural usefulness.

## 3. Elephant — Growth / Mass Regulation

**Likely classes:** regulatory + physiological

Possible expressions:
- increased size/mass
- thicker supporting tissues
- altered growth rate
- strength through scale

High-risk on small/light animals because structural/metabolic requirements may fail to keep up.

## 4. Gecko — Regeneration Suite

**Likely classes:** physiological + regulatory

Possible expressions:
- rapid wound repair
- tissue/limb regrowth
- scarless healing
- potentially adhesive surface traits as side expression

Expression strength should vary dramatically.

## 5. Cheetah — Fast-Twitch / Sprint Suite

**Likely classes:** physiological + regulatory

Possible expressions:
- explosive acceleration
- muscle fibre changes
- lighter/flexible body expression

Risks:
- overheating
- low endurance
- structural strain in heavy animals

## 6. Pangolin — Dermal Plate Suite

**Likely classes:** anatomical + physiological

Possible expressions:
- scale/plate growth
- keratin reinforcement
- defensive posture tendencies

Risks:
- reduced mobility
- interference with glands/fur/other surface expressions

## 7. Owl — Nocturnal Sensory Suite

**Likely classes:** sensory + anatomical/neurological

Possible expressions:
- low-light vision
- directional hearing
- head/eye changes
- improved target tracking

May produce visually odd eye/skull outcomes on unsuitable bases.

## 8. Electric Eel — Electrocyte Suite

**Likely classes:** biochemical + physiological

Possible expressions:
- electrical discharge
- electroreception
- altered conductive tissues

Risks:
- extreme metabolic load
- self-damage
- highly context-dependent usefulness

## 9. Toad — Toxin / Gland Suite

**Likely classes:** biochemical + anatomical

Possible expressions:
- defensive toxin secretion
- specialised glands
- altered skin texture

Risks:
- self-toxicity
- gland placement problems
- interference with other surface biology

## 10. Chameleon — Chromatophore / Visual Adaptation Suite

**Likely classes:** physiological + sensory + regulatory

Possible expressions:
- colour/pattern change
- camouflage
- unusual eye independence/tracking

Risks:
- unstable pigmentation
- poor visual integration
- energy cost

# Opening choice structure — PLANNED

Immediately after the disaster:

1. Player inspects the three surviving caged animals.
2. Player can inspect the ten surviving source records/material packages.
3. Existing SpliceMaster notes provide partial knowledge, not guaranteed outcomes.
4. Player chooses one base + one source package for the emergency first attempt.
5. The first attempt is weighted away from arbitrary instant death because it functions as tutorial/story progression, but still produces variable expression.
6. The resulting creature enters the imminent local Land Fit Pit bout.
7. Its exact outcome becomes part of that save's first persistent creature history.

The game should not tell the player that one starter/source combination is the “correct” choice.
