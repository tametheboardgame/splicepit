# SplicePit Work Packages — R2 / Alpha 2 / Act 2 Expansion

R2 proves that the systems and content model scale beyond the first chapter and that the paid/unpaid debt outcome creates genuinely different routes without requiring two entirely separate games.

---

## WP2A — Act 2 Branch Architecture and Content Lock

**Depends on:** R1 accepted.

**Purpose:** Define the scope of the free and creditor-controlled Act 2 routes before content production.

**Deliverables:**
- lock which story beats/regions are shared, branch-exclusive or reordered;
- define how and where the branches can reconverge without erasing consequences;
- lock creditor-route obligations/access/reward structure if not already final;
- define free-route specialisation choices;
- content manifest for new hubs, Pits, NPCs, animals, source packages and upgrades;
- preserve unresolved endgame mysteries explicitly.

**Gate:** every R2 content WP has a bounded target and branch ownership is clear.

## WP2B — Independent/Free Act 2 Route

**Depends on:** WP2A.

**Purpose:** Deliver the paid-debt route's promised autonomy.

**Deliverables:**
- open choice among approved regional/circuit/research/trade directions;
- quests and contacts that reward self-directed specialisation;
- route-specific Pit opportunities and biological sources;
- freedom to remain Land-focused or pursue Water/Air/mixed capability;
- consequences from Act 1 relationships and pit-development choices.

**Gate:** player can make at least two materially different progression choices early in Act 2 without the story forcing one canonical specialisation.

## WP2C — Creditor-Controlled Act 2 Route

**Depends on:** WP2A.

**Purpose:** Make default a substantial alternate campaign path rather than a penalty state.

**Deliverables:**
- assigned House/creditor fights and jobs;
- purse/reward deductions or obligation accounting as canon requires;
- constrained order/access balanced by unique contacts, transport, Pits and rare material;
- morally questionable work and internal creditor politics where authored;
- branch-specific characters and information;
- credible path toward reducing/clearing/restructuring the obligation.

**Gate:** unpaid-route player receives unique meaningful content and progression while experiencing reduced autonomy as an intentional narrative/mechanical difference.

## WP2D — Water, Air and Specialist Circuit Expansion

**Depends on:** WP2A–C, R1 Fit Pit content.

**Purpose:** Fully develop the environment-specialisation promise beyond Act 1 introductions.

**Deliverables:**
- substantial Water and Air Pit circuits;
- specialist trainers/handlers/housing/transport content;
- environment-specific opponents and hazards;
- dual- and multi-environment creature opportunities;
- specialist paths that remain viable without all-three coverage;
- generalist builds face systemic biological/action-economy trade-offs, not arbitrary penalties.

**Gate:** dedicated Land, Water, Air and mixed players each have viable Act 2 progression/content routes.

## WP2E — Advanced Biology, Rare Bases and Mutation Research Content

**Depends on:** WP2A, R0.3 systems, R1 biological roster.

**Purpose:** Expand experimental depth without replacing earlier content with strict higher tiers.

**Deliverables:**
- rare base animals with testing-scarcity consequences;
- new source packages across all biological classes;
- complex compatibility/epistasis interactions;
- mutation-derived sources/research quests;
- high-complexity work that rewards advanced diagnostics/safety rather than simply requiring level numbers;
- phenotype/capability assets for new content.

**Gate:** new biology creates qualitatively new strategies while common/early sources remain useful in some builds/testing contexts.

## WP2F — Advanced Pit, Training, Recovery and High-Risk Competition

**Depends on:** WP2D–E.

**Purpose:** Progress from comparatively safe competition into serious persistent-risk play.

**Deliverables:**
- higher-tier Pit rules with meaningful injury/loss risk;
- advanced recovery and training facility content;
- special/asymmetric/endurance bouts as authored;
- branch/circuit-specific prestigious or dangerous Pits;
- clear risk/reward communication;
- creature loss remains rare/foreseeable enough to preserve experimentation and attachment.

**Gate:** high-risk bouts feel materially different from early sparring and can produce persistent consequences without routine arbitrary deletion of main creatures.

## WP2G — Act 2 Progression, Economy and Branch Convergence Tuning

**Depends on:** WP2B–F.

**Purpose:** Keep the two routes economically and narratively viable while preserving their differences.

**Deliverables:**
- debt/independence economy tuning;
- acquisition/trading/rare-source pricing and availability;
- pit-upgrade continuation balance;
- branch-exclusive reward valuation;
- optional convergence/reconciliation points as authored;
- exploit/grind/save-retry analysis across both routes.

**Gate:** neither Act 2 branch is clearly the “correct” route for rewards/content, and both can progress without pathological grind.

## WP2H — Alpha 2 Integration, Scale, Save and Performance QA

**Depends on:** WP2A–G.

**Purpose:** Prove the project architecture scales beyond one chapter and one branch.

**Deliverables:**
- full Act 1 → Act 2 branch playthrough coverage;
- save migrations from Alpha 1 fixtures;
- large creature roster/test-stock/content-data stress tests;
- phenotype/cache/performance profiling;
- quest graph/branch blocker audit;
- balance simulation across expanded biology/circuits;
- browser/controller/accessibility/localisation checks;
- Alpha 2 known-issues/release record.

**Gate:** both major Act 2 routes are completable from legitimate Act 1 saves; new content is predominantly data/assets rather than engine rewrites; no high-severity save/progression/performance blocker remains.

### R2 release gate

SplicePit has demonstrated that its world, biology, combat and branch systems scale beyond the opening chapter without collapsing into deterministic recipes, one optimal circuit or bespoke code per piece of content.
