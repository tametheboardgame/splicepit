# SplicePit Work Packages — R1 / Alpha 1 / Complete Act 1

R1 is the first full authored chapter built on accepted R0 systems. Its purpose is not to invent new core engine concepts unless a genuine content blocker appears.

The Act 1 framework in `ACT1_STORY_FRAMEWORK.md` is planned structure; exact story, names, debt values and creditor details must be explicitly locked before the dependent WPs ship.

---

## WP1A — Act 1 Canon and Production Content Lock

**Depends on:** R0.7 accepted.

**Purpose:** Resolve all creative decisions that would otherwise cause content churn during Act 1 production.

**Required decisions/deliverables:**
- approve or replace the world-map structure/hub names used for Act 1;
- approve creditor faction identity/culture/Act 1 presence, including whether `The Clearing House` becomes canon;
- lock debt currency, amount, deadline model and default terms for Act 1;
- decide the immediate contractual reason the SpliceMaster's booked first bout must be honoured;
- lock Act 1 main NPC cast, local opponent(s), suspicion beats and chapter endpoints;
- approve/tune the relevant early pit-upgrade tree tiers;
- lock Act 1 geography/content manifest and explicit branch end states;
- record deliberately unresolved later-story mysteries separately rather than accidentally answering them.

**Gate:** no Act 1 content-producing WP depends on a PROVISIONAL identity, location, debt number or story beat.

## WP1B — Act 1 Geography and Environmental Content

**Depends on:** WP1A.

**Purpose:** Build the complete authored geography required for Act 1 at the agreed compact classic-RPG scale subset.

**Deliverables:**
- all Act 1 hubs/routes/side areas/interiors;
- traversal/collision/decorative layers;
- harvest pockets and environmental source opportunities;
- repeat-visit state changes;
- physical creature transport constraints represented where story/gameplay uses them;
- map-specific ambience/audio hooks;
- optional exploration that does not break the debt pacing.

**Gate:** all Act 1 destinations can be reached/returned from through normal play and maintain persistent state.

## WP1C — Act 1 NPCs, Dialogue, Relationships and Suspicion Arc

**Depends on:** WP1A–B.

**Purpose:** Populate the region with characters who react to the disaster, the player's work and the debt without prematurely resolving the wider mystery.

**Deliverables:**
- full Act 1 NPC definitions/dialogue/relationship states;
- varied reactions to the gas incident: belief, distrust, accusation, indifference and self-interest;
- creditor representatives if approved;
- traders, handlers, Pit staff, harvest/quest contacts and recurring characters;
- localisation-ready dialogue assets;
- branch-aware conversation variants.

**Gate:** NPC state remains coherent across quest progression, debt branch conditions and save/reload; no critical dialogue path dead-ends.

## WP1D — Act 1 Base Animals, Source Packages and Biological Content Roster

**Depends on:** WP1A, R0.3 accepted.

**Purpose:** Provide enough biological variety for experimentation to remain interesting throughout Act 1.

**Deliverables:**
- production-balanced Rabbit/Goat/Pig starter definitions;
- production definitions for the ten locked opening source packages;
- additional Act 1 common test animals and selected rare/quest animals;
- Act 1 harvestable/buyable/winnable/tradable source packages;
- phenotype components/capability expressions for the roster;
- compatibility/epistasis content sufficient to create both learnable and surprising interactions;
- research/diagnostic descriptions that do not expose hidden certainty.

**Gate:** Act 1 supports multiple materially different main-creature development paths without requiring one optimal starter/source route.

## WP1E — Act 1 Quests, Acquisition Economy and Optional Work

**Depends on:** WP1A–D, R0.5 accepted.

**Purpose:** Turn the region into an authored source of money, material, test animals and decisions rather than a linear corridor.

**Deliverables:**
- main quests for the planned Act 1 chapters;
- optional quests/jobs;
- natural introduction/use of Buy, Harvest, Win and Trade;
- source-animal rarity and common-test-animal availability tuned to support experimentation;
- quest rewards spanning money, material, access, information and upgrade components;
- anti-grind review so debt success is achievable through multiple play styles.

**Gate:** at least several viable debt-raising strategies exist and no one mandatory repetitive grind dominates normal play.

## WP1F — Act 1 Fit Pit Circuit and Water/Air Introduction

**Depends on:** WP1A, WP1D–E, R0.4 accepted.

**Purpose:** Build the real early competition structure around the capability-driven combat system.

**Deliverables:**
- local Land circuit from controlled early bouts to meaningful Act 1 competition;
- opponent builds/AI/personalities/rules/rewards;
- production Pit presentation and danger tiers;
- authored introduction to Water and Air opportunities without requiring competitive commitment;
- specialist/generalist routes remain viable;
- branch/story access rules for fights.

**Gate:** a Land-focused player can complete Act 1, while players who successfully develop Water/Air capability receive meaningful optional opportunities.

## WP1G — Act 1 Pit Upgrades, Training, Recovery and Creature Management

**Depends on:** WP1A, WP1D–F.

**Purpose:** Make the inherited pit visibly/mechanically develop through Act 1 and support attachment to main creatures.

**Deliverables:**
- approved early tiers across the nine upgrade domains;
- balanced upgrade costs/prerequisites;
- housing and test-stock capacity progression;
- training/practice and recovery content;
- sample storage and diagnostic progression;
- restoration states visible in the home base;
- main creature naming/history/training/injury inspection polished for normal play.

**Gate:** meaningful upgrade-order choices exist and no single branch is universally mandatory beyond true foundational prerequisites.

## WP1H — Debt Deadline and Paid/Unpaid Act 1 Resolution

**Depends on:** WP1A, WP1E–G.

**Purpose:** Deliver the defining Act 1 branch as gameplay rather than a cutscene toggle.

**Deliverables:**
- visible, understandable in-world deadline;
- partial payment/account state if approved;
- creditor warnings/escalation that do not create real-time anxiety outside the game clock;
- paid route resolution: claim released, autonomy preserved, independent Act 2 hooks;
- unpaid route resolution: no game over, contractual control/assigned-work state activated, unique Act 2 hooks;
- branch-specific rewards/relationships/access so unpaid is different content rather than less content;
- save state records branch cleanly.

**Gate:** both outcomes are reachable through legitimate play and produce stable distinct post-Act-1 states.

## WP1I — Alpha 1 Integration, Balance, Onboarding and QA

**Depends on:** WP1A–H.

**Purpose:** Make Act 1 independently playable by a new player and establish the first large save-compatibility/content baseline.

**Deliverables:**
- full new-game → Act 1 branch-end browser playthrough automation where practical;
- manual playtests with multiple starter/source/debt strategies;
- splice, combat, economy and pacing balance pass;
- quest/progression blocker audit;
- save/reload/migration coverage at chapter checkpoints;
- accessibility/controller/localisation layout checks;
- performance profiling with full Act 1 content;
- explicit Alpha 1 release notes and known limitations.

**Gate:** a fresh player can understand and complete Act 1 without external instructions; no known high-severity progression/save blocker remains; both debt outcomes have been completed from fresh saves.

### R1 release gate

Act 1 is a real chapter, not a vertical slice: it contains meaningful exploration, experimentation, creature development, competition, pit progression, social context and a branch that materially changes the start of Act 2.
