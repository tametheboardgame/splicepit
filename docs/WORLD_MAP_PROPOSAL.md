# World Map Proposal — NOT YET CANON

This is a structural draft for a first full-game-sized region. Names are placeholders and can be replaced wholesale.

## Scale target

Approximately:

- 8 major settlement/hub maps
- 18–22 connecting route maps
- 6–8 substantial side areas/dungeons/special harvest zones
- multiple small optional interiors/harvest pockets
- 3 overlapping Fit Pit circuits: Land, Water, Air

The target is a compact handcrafted world with roughly classic monster-RPG breadth, not a huge open world.

---

# High-level layout

All names are provisional.

---

# 1. Bramblewick — starting hub

Rural settlement built around patched farms, workshops and old infrastructure.

Contains:

- inherited SplicePit/lab on the edge of town
- first local Land pit
- basic animal sellers/holders
- common harvest opportunities
- people who knew the dead SpliceMaster
- immediate social suspicion after the disaster

Act 1 begins here.

Nearby routes should be rich in common test animals/material so the player quickly learns experimentation.

---

# 2. Latchmarket — trade hub

A busy semi-neutral market town where salvage, animals and biological material change hands.

Functions:

- genetic material buying/trading
- black-market contacts
- animal transport/handling gear
- quest board / travelling NPCs
- information about rare source animals
- access junction to several regions

This becomes the player's first taste of the wider splice economy.

---

# 3. Ironbank Quarry — Land specialist region

Quarry/mining settlement with hard terrain and a culture that values heavy impact creatures.

Functions:

- stronger Land circuit
- heavy animals/material
- structural/armour gene opportunities
- difficult terrain harvest areas
- workshop/salvage upgrade parts

A natural place to reward mass, armour, climbing and impact builds.

---

# 4. Bracken Fen — transition/harvest region

Marshland between ordinary Land territory and the Water circuit.

Functions:

- amphibious/wetland source animals
- toxins/glands/regeneration opportunities
- teaches environmental eligibility without forcing Water competition
- routes that change with water level/weather if desired later

---

# 5. Siltmere — Water specialist hub

Settlement around flooded infrastructure/lake/wetland systems.

Functions:

- Water Fit Pits
- aquatic animal access/trade
- specialist tanks/transport requirements
- electro/pressure/breathing/propulsion sources
- optional path for players who want a Water main creature

Core progression should allow a Land specialist to visit/use the town without being forced to win Water bouts.

---

# 6. Spillway — dam/industrial water region

A partially functional dam/reservoir and salvage settlement.

Functions:

- high-value aquatic/industrial quests
- dangerous electrical/water environments
- access to larger aquatic animals
- workshop/power upgrade components
- route toward Blackwater Works

---

# 7. Kestrel Ridge — Air specialist hub

High ridge/cliff settlement built around old towers, farms and wind infrastructure.

Functions:

- Air Fit Pits
- bird/bat/insect source opportunities
- flight housing/training specialists
- altitude/endurance/sensory material

Players can ignore competitive Air progression or specialise heavily here.

---

# 8. Crownless City — late central hub

Largest surviving urban centre in the region, fragmented among local interests rather than functioning as a strong central government.

Functions:

- high-tier pit organisers
- major trade
- creditor/criminal/political actors
- access to multiple circuits
- advanced equipment/services
- late-story branch convergence/divergence

It should feel larger and more dangerous politically, not like a restored modern capital.

---

# Major side areas

## Old Aerodrome

Abandoned aviation/warehouse zone above Latchmarket route network.

- Air source material
- salvage
- dangerous vertical spaces
- possible training/transport hardware

## Glasshouse Ruins

Former biological research/agricultural facility now overgrown/broken.

- rare records
- unusual regulatory/mutation material
- environmental storytelling about pre-collapse biotech
- high-risk harvesting

## Blackwater Works

Old industrial/water-treatment complex.

- biochemical/electrical material
- toxins
- specialist reagents
- infrastructure parts

## Quarry Depths

Optional deeper Ironbank area.

- large/armoured source animals
- rare minerals/equipment
- dangerous confined Land encounters

## The Flooded Transit

Submerged old transport route near Siltmere/Crownless City.

- advanced Water traversal/harvest
- optional route/shortcuts

## Ridge Caves

Cliff/cave network under Kestrel Ridge.

- bats/sensory sources
- connects Air region to ground exploration

# Act structure across geography

## Act 1 — Debt clock

Primary accessible area:

- Bramblewick
- nearby common routes
- Latchmarket
- at least one of Ironbank / Bracken Fen depending progression

The player has multiple legitimate ways to raise money rather than one mandatory grind.

At the deadline, debt paid/unpaid creates the Act 2 branch.

## Act 2A — Free path (debt paid)

Player can choose emphasis:

- Land circuit toward Ironbank
- Water exploration toward Siltmere
- Air circuit toward Kestrel Ridge
- trading/research routes through Latchmarket/Glasshouse

## Act 2B — Creditor path (debt unpaid)

The same world remains accessible but creditor jobs/fights constrain order and create different opportunities/relationships.

This branch should expose content unavailable to the free path as compensation for reduced agency, rather than simply punish the player with less game.

## Later game

Routes converge toward Crownless City and higher-tier pit/creditor/research conflicts while preserving specialisation choices.

---

# Design rules

1. No circuit should require owning one of each Land/Water/Air main creature just to complete the core story.
2. Specialist players should have viable progression and unique opportunities.
3. Common source animals remain useful for testing everywhere.
4. Rare harvest sites should encourage planning/quests rather than random grinding.
5. Returning to earlier hubs should remain useful through trade, research, pit upgrades and new access.
6. The map must support physical creature transport fiction; bringing a huge pig-elephant monstrosity across the region should not feel like pulling it from a pocket.
