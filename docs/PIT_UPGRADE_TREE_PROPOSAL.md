# Pit Upgrade Tree Proposal — NOT YET CANON

## Design rule

Pit upgrades should mainly change **what the player can know, attempt, recover, store, house or train**, rather than act as a wall of flat +5% bonuses.

The tree has nine branches. Exact costs/tiers are later balance work.

---

# Foundation prerequisites

## F0 — Emergency Utilities

Restore safe-enough power, water and ventilation after the opening disaster.

**Effect:** allows basic lab operation.

## F1 — Containment Repairs

Repair doors, cage latches and gas isolation controls.

**Effect:** unlocks larger test-animal stock and safer handling of unstable creatures.

## F2 — Functional Lab

Clean/repair surfaces, drains, lighting and core work area.

**Effect:** prerequisite for most Tier 2 research/safety upgrades.

---

# Branch A — Diagnostics

## D1 — Basic Microscope & Record Bench

**Unlocks:** structured experiment records; reveals observed expression results/ranges from prior tests.

## D2 — Salvaged Sequencer

**Unlocks:** identifies broad source classes/tags and obvious incompatibility markers before commitment.

## D3 — Comparative Scanner

**Unlocks:** compare prior results across base-animal types; highlights base-specific warning patterns.

## D4 — Epistasis Workstation

**Unlocks:** better visibility of likely multi-source synergy/conflict and narrower probability bands.

## D5 — Predictive Biology Suite

**Unlocks:** highest-quality pre-splice forecasts and confidence ranges available to the player.

**Important:** never guarantees exact expression.

---

# Branch B — Splice Safety & Precision

## S1 — Sterile Delivery Rig

**Effect:** reduces contamination/avoidable damaging failure; enables cleaner sample handling.

## S2 — Precision Insertion Tools

**Effect:** allows the player to bias an attempt toward a known expression class/target region when their knowledge supports it.

## S3 — Live Monitoring Array

**Effect:** detects some dangerous failure trajectories during the attempt; may turn selected catastrophic paths into aborted/rejected attempts at resource cost.

## S4 — Staged Integration Rig

**Effect:** permits selected complex work to be divided into monitored stages, providing information and intervention points.

## S5 — Isolation Splice Chamber

**Effect:** enables experiments the ordinary bench considers too dangerous/unstable to attempt; does not make them safe.

---

# Branch C — Animal Recovery

## R1 — Treatment Pen

**Unlocks:** faster recovery from ordinary battle/splice trauma; basic wound treatment.

## R2 — Surgical Bench

**Unlocks:** treatment of selected persistent injuries; better diagnosis of damaged anatomy.

## R3 — Metabolic Recovery Suite

**Unlocks:** specialised recovery for exhaustion, toxicity and unstable metabolic expression.

## R4 — Tissue Recovery Tank

**Unlocks:** attempts to recover severe tissue damage using controlled regenerative techniques/material.

## R5 — Intensive Containment Ward

**Unlocks:** keeps critically unstable/dangerous creatures recoverable/observable after outcomes that would otherwise force immediate retirement/euthanasia/death checks.

---

# Branch D — Sample Storage

## SS1 — Working Refrigerator

**Effect:** basic sample capacity and reduced spoilage.

## SS2 — Cryogenic Chest

**Effect:** long-term preservation; increased capacity for rare material.

## SS3 — Indexed Sample Bank

**Effect:** proper metadata/lot tracking; separates source batches and connects material to experiment records.

## SS4 — Specialist Containment Storage

**Effect:** safely stores toxic, unstable, electrical or otherwise awkward biological material.

## SS5 — Archive Vault

**Effect:** preserves tiny/rare mutation-derived and unique samples that ordinary storage cannot keep viable; substantially improves long-term collection strategy without creating infinite material.

---

# Branch E — Mutation Analysis

Requires Diagnostics progression.

## M1 — Pathology Station

**Unlocks:** identify that an observed trait is mutation/side-effect rather than intended expression.

## M2 — Mutation Scanner

**Unlocks:** categorises mutation behaviour and predicts whether it is stable enough to investigate.

## M3 — Stabilisation Rig

**Unlocks:** attempt to stabilise a mutation in its current creature. Attempt may fail or alter it.

## M4 — Extraction Bench

**Unlocks:** attempt to harvest mutation-derived material for preservation/testing.

## M5 — Culture Chamber

**Unlocks:** attempt to reproduce/cultivate a mutation-derived biological source outside the original creature using scarce material/reagents. Never a guaranteed infinite-copy machine.

---

# Branch F — Creature Housing

## H1 — Repaired Cages

**Effect:** small test-animal stock capacity.

## H2 — Proper Pens

**Effect:** larger/common animal housing; reduces routine stress/injury/escape events.

## H3 — Reinforced Main-Creature Bays

**Effect:** safe accommodation for heavily modified/large/dangerous main creatures.

## H4 — Specialist Habitat Modules

Choose/build modules such as:
- aquatic tank
- flight/aviary space
- heavy reinforced pen
- temperature/humidity controlled habitat

**Effect:** enables proper housing/recovery/testing for specialised animals.

## H5 — Expanded Animal Wing

**Effect:** substantially larger test-stock capacity and multiple specialist habitat modules.

**Main combat roster remains capped at three.**

---

# Branch G — Fit Pit & Training Facilities

## P1 — Practice Pen

**Unlocks:** safe basic capability practice with no formal bout.

## P2 — Training Equipment

**Unlocks:** train reliability/discipline with anatomy already possessed: charge lanes, bite targets, balance rigs, etc.

## P3 — Controlled Sparring Pit

**Unlocks:** low-risk practice fights and controlled test matchups at home.

## P4 — Environment Training Modules

Supports specialist practice: water tank/current rig, flight frame/airspace, variable land obstacles.

## P5 — Advanced Training Pit

**Unlocks:** more complex multi-creature/asymmetric training and higher-level AI/training routines without paying external pit fees.

---

# Branch H — Workshop / Fabrication

## W1 — Salvage Bench

**Unlocks:** convert junk/quest salvage into basic lab/containment components.

## W2 — Fabrication Tools

**Unlocks:** craft repair parts, cages, transport hardware and selected reagents/equipment components.

## W3 — Custom Restraint & Handling Gear

**Unlocks:** better leads, muzzles, harnesses, transport cages and specialist handling equipment for modified creatures.

## W4 — Transport Rigging

**Unlocks:** safer/easier movement of large, aquatic or awkward main creatures between regions/pits.

## W5 — Bespoke Biological Hardware

**Unlocks:** custom apparatus required by certain high-end experiments/quests rather than purchasing everything outright.

---

# Branch I — Cosmetic / Public Restoration

Cosmetic work should sometimes have social effects but not secretly become mandatory combat power.

## C1 — Clean the Blood Off Everything

Restore the lab from “fresh mass casualty scene” to “questionable place of business”.

**Effect:** visual restoration; allows selected visitors inside without immediate horror reaction.

## C2 — Roof, Walls & Windows

**Effect:** major visual improvement; weather-proofing; unlocks some rooms/space.

## C3 — Proper Signage & Frontage

**Effect:** changes external appearance/reputation; improves ordinary customer/trader access.

## C4 — Public Receiving Area

**Effect:** traders/NPCs can visit; enables selected deals/quests at the pit.

## C5 — Restored SplicePit

**Effect:** high-status visual transformation reflecting player identity/path; potential reputation/story hooks, but not a universal +combat stat.

---

# Dependency examples

- Mutation Stabilisation requires Diagnostics + Functional Lab.
- Specialist aquatic/air training requires matching Housing/Workshop capability.
- Extreme Splice Chamber requires advanced Safety + Containment Repairs.
- Rare sample archive requires Cryogenic storage + reliable utilities.
- High-end recovery may require mutation/diagnostic knowledge depending on injury.

The tree should contain meaningful cross-links so the player cannot simply max one branch without supporting infrastructure.

# Upgrade philosophy

A player specialising in experimental biology may rush Diagnostics/Mutation/Safety.
A fighter may prioritise Recovery/Training/Housing.
A trader/collector may prioritise Storage/Workshop/Housing.
All paths eventually benefit from the others, but their order should be a strategic choice.
